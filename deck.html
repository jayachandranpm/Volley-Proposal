Deliverable 3 · Testing plan

Run, read,
and kill.

What I'm not optimizing

Not CPT. Not even ROAS in the first 30 days. A $25 trial that churns in 30 days is more expensive than a $45 trial that pays for six months.

Most paid teams nurture losers because they're emotionally invested in the creative. The job is to be ruthless.

What I am optimizing

LTV ÷ CAC at Day 90. Every kill rule, every metric tier, every audience choice filters out cheap-but-bad traffic.

Hard kill: hook rate < 15% by Day 3 → dead. No exceptions, regardless of CTR.

Refused the obvious,

Where I refused to hunt

Where I did hunt

People who already sell exactly this product at a competitor. Founder-operators who've built the same engine on the brand side. Sales-led founders whose pitch language is already deal-shaped.

The plan after
the plan.
