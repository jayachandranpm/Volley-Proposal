# Volley Meta Ads Creative Strategy & Testing Plan

> **Asset note:** All hero imagery is sourced from Unsplash (real photography, royalty-free) and composited with flat UI overlays in Figma. No AI-generated imagery — the entire pitch leans on the viewer recognising real moments, and synthetic photography breaks that recognition.

## Overview & Testing Plan
**Objective:** Identify the lowest Cost Per Trial (CPT) by testing three distinct psychological hooks and target audiences.

1. **Phase 1: Angle Test (Days 1-7)**
 - 1 Broad CBO Campaign, 3 Ad Sets (one per angle).
 - Determine which hook grabs attention and qualified clicks.
2. **Phase 2: Format Test (Days 8-14)**
 - Take the winning angle and iterate on format (e.g., UGC vs. Motion Graphics vs. Static).
3. **Phase 3: Scale & Optimize (Days 15+)**
 - Scale the winner and optimize the post-click onboarding funnel to decrease CPT. Kill creatives with <15% hook rate or high drop-off.

---

## Angle 1: The "App Dropout" (The Anti-Streak Hook)
**Audience:** Serial language app downloaders, Duolingo churners, people who have tried to learn 3 times but still can't speak.
**The Promise:** Accountability and actual fluency, not just gamified dopamine.

* **Visual Direction (UGC Video/Static):** POV selfie style. The creator is holding up a smartphone showing a 300-day streak on a cartoon owl app, looking dead inside.
* **Video/Static Hook:** "I had a 300-day streak on [App] and I still couldn't order a coffee in Spanish."
* **Body Copy:** Ditch the toxic streaks. Combine unlimited AI speaking practice with a real human tutor who holds you to it. Speak from week one.
* **Headline:** Learn for real this time.
* **CTA:** Start Free Trial

### Visual Sourcing
> **Unsplash search:** `frustrated young person phone`, `tired millennial smartphone`, `bored adult mobile screen`. Pick a POV-style or close-crop selfie shot with natural lighting and an unpolished feel — the streak counter and app UI are added in post as a flat overlay so the photo reads as authentically *found*, not staged.

## Angle 2: The "Busy Professional" (The ROI of Time Hook)
**Audience:** High-income earners, expats, professionals, 30-45 age bracket.
**The Promise:** You only need 15 minutes a day and one 30-minute call a week to make massive progress.

* **Visual Direction (Motion Graphic / Static):** A sleek, split-screen visual. Left side: A packed Google Calendar. Right side: A quick, 3-message audio chat with the Volley AI. The text overlays: "How busy people learn French."
* **Primary Text:** Short on time? Practice when you have 5 minutes. Live tutor call when you have 30.
* **Headline:** Fluent, fitted to your schedule.
* **CTA:** Learn More

### Visual Sourcing
> **Unsplash search:** `busy calendar laptop`, `professional desk planner`, `executive schedule`. Use a clean, well-lit shot of a packed calendar or planner as the left half; the right half is a flat-rendered Volley chat UI. Both sides composited in Figma against a soft neutral background — the photo carries the "real life" weight so the UI feels like the relief.

---

## Angle 3: The "Real-World Prep" (The Hyper-Specific Hook)
**Audience:** Upcoming travelers, digital nomads, deep-interest learners.
**The Promise:** Practice the *exact* conversations you're about to have in the real world, before you have them.

* **Visual Direction (UGC/B-Roll Hybrid):** B-Roll of someone nervously sitting at a Parisian cafe. On-screen UI mocks up the Volley green-bubble chat interface overlaying the video.
* **Video/Static Hook:** [Text on screen: "Practicing exactly how to order at a Parisian cafe... without the anxiety."]
* **Body Copy:** Ordering a coffee shouldn't feel like a pop quiz. Practice real dialogue with a voice AI before stepping out.
* **Headline:** Crush your language anxiety.
* **CTA:** Start Free Trial

### Visual Sourcing
> **Unsplash search:** `Parisian cafe table`, `cafe menu travel`, `solo traveler cafe France`. Pick a warm, slightly-out-of-focus 35mm-style shot — ideally from a sitting POV with a menu or coffee in frame. The Volley green-bubble chat overlay is layered on in Figma. Real photography is the point: AI cafes look like AI cafes, and the entire angle relies on the viewer recognising the moment as one they've actually lived.
