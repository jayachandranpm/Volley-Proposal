After two years of building apps with React Native, I made the switch to Flutter. Here is what changed and why I am never going back.

## The Good Parts

- **Hot reload** actually works reliably
- Single codebase for iOS, Android, Web, Desktop
- Dart is a joy to write
- No bridge — direct compilation to native

## What I Miss

- The JavaScript ecosystem breadth
- Some niche packages

## Conclusion

Flutter is the future of cross-platform development.
